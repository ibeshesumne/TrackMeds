# Privacy Policy for TrackMeds

**Last updated:** March 2026

## Overview

TrackMeds is a medication tracking app. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Store

TrackMeds stores the following data on your device:

- Medication names, dosages, schedules, and start/end dates
- Daily records: doses taken, weight, exercise duration, symptoms, and side effects

## Where Your Data Is Stored

**All data is stored locally on your device only.** We do not send your data to any external servers. No cloud sync, no analytics, no third-party services receive your health information.

## Data Collection

TrackMeds does not collect data from you. You enter information directly into the app, and it remains on your device.

## Export and Import

- **Export:** You can export your data to JSON or CSV files. Export is user-initiated only. Files are saved to your device or shared at your choice.
- **Import:** You can import data from files you choose. Imported data stays on your device.

## Your Rights

You can:

- **Access** your data at any time within the app
- **Export** your data to JSON or CSV
- **Delete** your data using the Delete options in the Export/Import section
- **Delete the app** to remove all data from your device

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
